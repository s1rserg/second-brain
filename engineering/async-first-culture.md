# My Rules for Async-First Engineering Culture

## Kill Status Update Meetings
I have a strict rule: if a meeting exists solely for people to recite what they did yesterday, it shouldn’t exist.
- Standups longer than 10 minutes are a sign of broken written communication.
- If you need feedback on architecture, write an RFC doc first. Give the team 24 hours to leave inline comments before even considering booking a 30-minute sync.
- The highest-leverage engineers I know are rarely the loudest on calls; they write clear, concise documentation that unblocks people across time zones.

## The Cost of Context Switching
Every Slack notification is a micro-interruption that takes 15–20 minutes of focus time to recover from.
- We batch Slack replies 2–3 times a day instead of keeping chat open on a second monitor.
- If an issue is truly a P0 fire, call or page. If it's not a P0, it can wait 2 hours for a thoughtful answer rather than an instant, half-baked reaction.
